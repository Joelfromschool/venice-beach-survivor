Privacy Policy

Venice Beach Survivor by AMSA Baby Productions
Last updated: October 15, 2026

Summary

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What the game stores

Your progress (unlocked fighters, coins, settings, and save slot) is stored only on your device using the browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app or clearing website data removes it.

Third parties

The game makes no network requests to third-party services during play.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
