🎃 Halloween night. Pooh went trick-or-treating, followed a candy trail into the woods… and wandered right up to Asmongold's house.
🏠 All Games

How to Play

Goal: fill Pooh's pumpkin tote with at least 30 candies, find the gate key in Asmongold's backyard safe, and escape through the front gate to get home.

You are not a fighter. Sneak, hide, and use your flashlight wisely. If Asmongold spots you he'll chase you — break line of sight and hide (closets, under the futon, behind the couch, the bathtub, the box fort). If he catches you, he keeps 5 candies in his candy bowl (you can steal them back!).

👁 Eye meter = how close Asmon is to noticing you. Sneaking and lights-off make you harder to see. Running is loud!
🔴 Stream time: Asmon sits at his desk and streams with Dark Kermit. Chat can see you on the webcam…
Shadow trick-or-treaters only move when you're NOT looking at them. Keep them in view.
🏺 Relic hunt: 28 Asmongold lore relics glow faintly gold around the house and yard (one is up in the treehouse — climb the ladder!). Some only show up during events, in the ☀️ daytime replay, or after cooking and building the igloo. Every 5 relics gives a reward, and finding them ALL unlocks a bonus ending. Progress is saved.
🍔 Tier list: walk up to Asmon's desk while he's LIVE and you might end up making a fast food tier list together…

Desktop: WASD move · mouse look (click to lock) · Shift run · C/Ctrl sneak · F flashlight · E use/hide · Space jump · G float · B boo · V camera · Tab bag · Esc pause
👻 Ghost powers: G / FLOAT = a short silent float. B / BOO = startle Mr Bean so he makes a racket somewhere else (Asmon goes to check).
🍬 Candy is money: talk to a friend again to trade candy for upgrades. Pooh haggles!
🚪 Knock: trick-or-treat at room doors. Candy… or a very awake Asmon.

Phone: drag the left side to move, drag the right side to look, big buttons for Use / Run / Sneak / Light / Float / Boo.
