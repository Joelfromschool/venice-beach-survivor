How to Play

GoalFill Pooh's bucket with 30 candies, find the gate key in the backyard safe, and escape through the front gate.
MoveDrag on the left side to walk. Drag on the right side to look around.
UseTap ✋ to pick up, open, talk and hide. It glows when something is nearby.
Spotted?Not game over! Tap the big HIDE button fast. A still sheet is just a decoration.
More movesTap ••• for run, sneak, jump, float and boo. Sneaking with the light off keeps you hidden.
ExploreRelics glow gold. New places give candy. The map and mini-games are in the pause menu.
