# Space Maze: art request (2 sheets)
Save the files to space/art/, then run `python3 slice_space.py`. Every sprite falls back to the built-in art until its sheet exists.

Style for both sheets: the same painted look as the Venice Beach SoR4 art. Hand-painted 2D game sprites with thick dark ink outlines, rich shading, warm rim light from the upper left and a soft pink/lavender rim from the right. Cute, family-friendly cartoon. Each character fills about 85% of its cell, centred, with no text, no shadows on the ground and nothing crossing the cell borders. The background must be flat pure magenta #FF00FF with no gradient. Equal grid with no gutters or borders. JPG or PNG.

## Sheet 1: space_sheet1.jpg (4 columns x 3 rows, for example 2048x1536 with 512x512 cells)
Row 1, the hero: Mr Bean (brown tweed jacket, white shirt collar, thin red tie, dark trousers, dark brown side-parted hair) riding on Winnie-the-Pooh's back (golden fur, red shirt) in space, flying away from the camera:
1. hero_back: seen from behind and slightly above. Pooh's back and the back of his head with ears, Mr Bean sitting on top, holding Pooh's ears and grinning back over his right shoulder so his face is visible.
2. hero_left: the same, banking left with the whole pair tilted about 20° left.
3. hero_right: the same, banking right.
4. hero_bump: Pooh doing a forward headbutt with his head ducked forward, and Mr Bean hanging on with his eyes squeezed shut and his legs flying up.
Row 2, bosses, front view, grumpy but cute:
5. boss_cloud: Grumpy Cloud Monster (big white puffy cloud, angry eyebrows, pouty mouth).
6. boss_blob: Wobbly Blob Gloop (translucent lime-green jelly blob, wobbly, cheeky frown).
7. boss_robot: Cranky Robo-Sat 3000 (boxy silver-white robot satellite, blue solar-panel arms, antenna, grumpy LED eyes).
8. boss_dragon: Sulky Comet Dragon (coral-orange chubby dragon head with cream horns and a flaming comet tail).
Row 3:
9. boss_moon: Sleepy Moon-Face (pale-yellow round moon face, purple nightcap with white pompom, grumpy and half-asleep).
10. alien: friendly little green alien in a mini silver saucer, waving.
11. jelly: cute pink/purple space jellyfish with a smile and glowing tentacles.
12. comet: cute small comet with a surprised face and a short golden flame tail.

## Sheet 2: space_sheet2.jpg (3 columns x 2 rows, for example 1536x1024 with 512x512 cells)
1. star: chunky glossy golden 5-point star, collectible style.
2. satellite: small white satellite with blue panels and a dish.
3. whale: big friendly blue space whale, side view, facing right, starry belly.
4. planet: pastel ringed planet with a pink/teal swirl.
5. asteroid: soft lumpy lavender-grey asteroid, cute rather than scary.
6. fluff: a fluffy pink-white fluff ball (the bosses' harmless projectile).

The total is 2 sheets and 18 sprites. Nothing else needs art: UI, portal, rings and particles stay code.
